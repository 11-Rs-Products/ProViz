Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026 · Applies to ProViz, operated by 11Rs Products

The short version

  • Your code runs in your browser and is never uploaded to us.
  • If you sign in, we receive your name, e-mail address and profile picture from Google — nothing else.
  • We do not run advertising, sell personal data or use third-party analytics.
  • You can use the Visualizer as a guest without giving us any personal information.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you choose “Continue with Google”, authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We receive your display name, e-mail address, profile picture URL and a unique account identifier. We use your e-mail address to decide which role your account has: General access for everyone, or Admin / Super admin developer access for the ProViz team.

Guest use. If you continue without signing in, we do not collect any personal information. A single flag is stored in your browser so ProViz remembers you chose guest mode.

Technical data. Like any website, the servers that deliver ProViz and its resources receive standard request information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. We do not combine this with your account.

2. Your code

Programs you write in ProViz are executed by a WebAssembly build of the language runtime (for Python, Pyodide) inside your browser tab. Your source code, program output and execution traces stay on your device and are not transmitted to 11Rs Products. If you export an audit trail, the file is generated locally and saved only where you choose.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in across visits.
  • To determine your role and show the studios that role is allowed to use.
  • To display your name and picture in the app so you can see which account is active.
  • To keep the service secure and to investigate abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decisions with legal effect, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Cookies & local storage

ProViz does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a small amount of data in your browser’s local storage to make the app work:

ItemPurposeLifetime
proviz.themeRemembers light or dark themeUntil cleared
proviz.studio, layout and speed preferencesRestores your last studio, pane sizes and playback speedUntil cleared
proviz.guestRemembers that you chose guest modeUntil you sign in or out
Firebase Authentication sessionKeeps you signed inUntil you sign out

You can clear all of this at any time through your browser settings or by signing out.

5. Third-party services

ProViz relies on a small number of providers. Each receives only what it needs to deliver its part of the service and processes it under its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase Authentication — sign-in with Google accounts.
  • jsDelivr CDN — delivers the language runtime files (Pyodide for Python).
  • Google Fonts — delivers the Inter, Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono typefaces.
  • Our hosting provider — serves the ProViz web pages.

6. Retention & deletion

Account records are kept by Firebase Authentication for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account record at any time using the contact details below; we will complete the request within 30 days. Information stored in your browser is under your control and can be removed by clearing site data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict how it is used. We honour these requests for every user regardless of location. To make a request, contact us as described below.

8. Children

ProViz is a general-audience developer tool. Users under the age required to consent to data processing in their country should use guest mode or sign in only with permission from a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app before they take effect.

10. Contact

ProViz is designed, built and operated by 11Rs Products. For privacy questions or requests, open an issue on our GitHub repository or reach us through the 11Rs Products organisation page. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue — ask us for a private channel instead.
