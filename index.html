Questions, answered.

The short version of how ProViz works and what it does with your code.

Still have a question? Ask us on GitHub
Do I need an account?

No. Choose “Try without signing in” to use the Visualizer straight away. Signing in with any Google account adds your profile to the app and keeps you signed in between visits.

Which languages are supported?

ProViz currently supports Python — standard CPython compiled to WebAssembly, including the standard library. Java, C++ and JavaScript are planned for future updates. Interactive input() is not supported yet.

Is my code uploaded anywhere?

No. Execution and tracing happen locally in your browser tab. The only network requests are for the language runtime, fonts and, if you sign in, Google authentication.

What happens if my program loops forever?

ProViz stops execution safely after a step and time budget, then lets you inspect everything that happened up to that point.

Does it work on tablets and phones?

Yes. On smaller screens the workspace stacks the editor, playback controls and 3D view vertically, and the 3D view supports touch to orbit and zoom. A larger screen is more comfortable for longer programs.

Is ProViz free?

Yes. The Visualizer and debugger are free to use, signed in or as a guest.
